Our Story

Since 2014, an ode to tradition worn with modern grace

Shosta Bazar BD is the exclusive luxury fashion house for women in Bangladesh. Our collection showcases a perfect blend of modern and traditional designs, featuring hand-placed embellishments, sequin and mirror work, and richly textured fabrics.

Every piece is finished by hand in our Dhaka atelier — from bridal lehengas to occasion sarees — so that what you wear feels as considered as the moment you wear it for.
